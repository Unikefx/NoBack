How to Export Followers & Following Data

Tutorial by @unik_efx

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open Instagram and go to Settings & Privacy > Accounts Center.
  2. Select Your Information and Permissions > Download Your Information.
  3. Click Download or Transfer Information > Some of your information.
  4. Scroll down and check Followers and Following.
  5. Select format HTML (or JSON) and request your file.
  6. Extract the `.zip` file and find followers.html and following.html.
  7. Drag & drop both files into the boxes above!
